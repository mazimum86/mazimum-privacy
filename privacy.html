1. Who We Are

MazimumQuotes is developed and published by Mazimum, an independent developer based in Nigeria. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at chukwuka.jerry@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect and How

MazimumQuotes is designed to work entirely offline. The following information is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to Mazimum's servers:

  • Your display name and handle (entered during onboarding)
  • Your chosen vibe and app preferences
  • Quotes you mark as favourites
  • Personal quotes, notes, and attribution you write and save
  • Images you generate and save to your gallery
  • Your daily streak and usage statistics
All of the above is stored in a local Room database on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of this data.

3. Third-Party Services

While Mazimum itself does not collect personal data, certain third-party services integrated into the app may do so independently.

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve relevant ads. This data is governed by Google's own privacy policy. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy . You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Google ML Kit (Face Detection)

When you choose a photo with a face, the app uses Google ML Kit's on-device face detection to intelligently position the quote text. This processing happens entirely on your device. No photo or biometric data is sent to Google or Mazimum.

Google Play In-App Review API

After you create several quote images, the app may ask you to rate it using the Google Play in-app review flow. This is handled entirely by Google Play and Mazimum does not receive or store your review content directly.

Google Play Billing (Pro subscription)

If you purchase a Pro subscription, payment is processed by Google Play. Mazimum does not handle or store any payment information.

4. App Permissions

MazimumQuotes requests the following device permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — to let you choose a photo from your gallery as a quote background. Images are processed locally and never uploaded.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 9 and below only) — to save generated quote images to your Pictures folder.
  • INTERNET — required for AdMob to serve ads and for the in-app review flow. Not used for any other purpose.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restart the daily widget refresh schedule when your device reboots.

5. Personal Quotes You Write

MazimumQuotes allows you to write and save your own personal quotes — from sermons, movies, conversations, scripture, or your own thoughts. These quotes and any private notes you attach to them are stored exclusively in your device's local database.

Personal quotes are never backed up to the cloud, never shared with Mazimum, and never visible to other users. They exist only on your device and are deleted permanently when you uninstall the app.

6. Quote Images You Create

Images generated by the app are saved directly to your device's gallery folder (Pictures/MazimumQuotes). When you share an image using the share sheet, you are sharing it through your chosen platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, etc.) according to that platform's own privacy policy. Mazimum does not receive a copy of shared images.

7. Children's Privacy

MazimumQuotes is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, please contact us at chukwuka.jerry@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by MazimumQuotes lives on your device. There is no account, no server-side profile, and no cloud backup.

To delete all your data, simply uninstall the app. This permanently removes your display name, preferences, favourites, personal quotes, and all notes from your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy — for example, if we add a feature that sends data to a server — we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify users through an in-app notice on the next app update.
